Most owners we onboard are leaving money on the table — not through anything
exotic, just through expenses that never made it into the books.

## 1. Home office proportion

If you work from home, a proportion of rent, utilities and internet is
deductible. The proportion has to be defensible: floor area used exclusively
for work, divided by total floor area, is the calculation that survives review.

**Keep:** a floor plan with measurements, and twelve months of utility bills.
